# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + GSAP + Lucide icons. Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-23; motion-driven single-page marketing site, deployed nowhere (local verification only).

## Users

Prospective clients and partners — founders, product leaders, and marketing/brand directors at companies that need a website, digital experience, product build, or creative-technology engagement. They arrive from a link, a referral, or a search, and their job is to judge within seconds whether this studio is technically serious enough to trust with their project, then start an enquiry. Secondary audience: design/engineering peers scanning for craft quality.

## Product Purpose

SLATE's official website. It exists to make the studio legible — what it makes, how it thinks, and how it works — and to convert that understanding into a project enquiry. Success means a visitor finishes with an accurate model of SLATE's capability and a reason to contact it; the site itself is also a demonstration of the studio's design and engineering standard.

## Positioning

SLATE is an independent digital design and technology studio: design and engineering under one roof, at senior level, without agency overhead. The distinguishing claim is not a category difference (others also do websites and products) but a working standard — editorial typographic rigor, deliberate motion, and engineering quality (performance, accessibility, semantics) treated as one craft, not as separate services.

## Operating Context

Single long-form page composed of authored sections: Navigation, Hero, Selected Work, Services, Studio, Process, Contact. No CMS, no backend, no forms that submit anywhere in this build. Site copy is authored in the studio voice: confident, minimal, precise, no hype.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No invented clients, testimonials, awards, partnerships, statistics, or project results — this is a hard product constraint, not a style preference.
- Selected-work entries are conceptual projects and must be visibly labeled as concepts, never presented as commissioned client work.
- No placeholder lorem ipsum; every string on the page is real authored copy.
- No dead buttons: every CTA either navigates to a real anchor/section or a clearly-marked placeholder contact channel.
- Contact details are placeholders by intent (no real address or phone on hand).
- Sections required and named by the brief: Work, Services, Studio, Contact, plus Process, plus an availability indicator in navigation.
- Motion budget: deliberate, timed, eased; no cheap zoom effects; no animation that compromises usability or performance.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must be honored across all motion.
- No horizontal overflow at any breakpoint; no console or runtime errors; no broken navigation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: SLATE. Wordmark treatment is a binding identity element.
- Positioning line: independent digital design & technology studio.
- Personality (binding): confident, minimal, experimental, intelligent, precise, contemporary, technically sophisticated.
- Must read as a serious digital studio — explicitly not a freelancer portfolio and not a template-based web agency.
- Tonal constraints (binding): no generic purple gradients, no standard SaaS landing-page structure, no excessive glassmorphism, no random floating cards, no unnecessary rounded containers, no emoji, no generic AI-agency aesthetic.
- Direction references (binding): Swiss editorial typography, modern brutalism, sophisticated whitespace, asymmetric layouts, oversized typography, restrained expressive color, experimental grids, crafted micro-interactions, cinematic transitions.
- Hero headline direction: "WE MAKE DIGITAL MATTER." with the given supporting copy.
- Closing headline direction: "HAVE SOMETHING IN MIND?"
- Services taxonomy (binding): 01 Digital Experiences, 02 Website Design & Development, 03 Product Development, 04 Creative Technology, 05 Digital Strategy.
- Process phases (binding): 01 Discover, 02 Define, 03 Design, 04 Develop, 05 Deliver.

## Evidence on Hand

None. The project directory was empty at start. There are no real client projects, case studies, brand assets, logos, photography, or copy decks on hand. All visual treatments must be authored/generated as original design artifacts, and all project entries must be framed as concepts. Future work must not fill this gap with fabricated client names, results, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over invention. SLATE's site must be truthful to the point where it is interesting; a reader should never be able to catch a fabricated claim.
2. One deliberate idea per screen. A single memorable element beats a dozen competent ones; restraint is the brand.
3. Craft is the argument. Motion, typography, and code quality are the proof of capability, so polish the details a visitor will never consciously notice.
4. Performance is a design decision. Motion never blocks comprehension, and reduced-motion users get an equal experience.
5. Engineered, not templated. Maintainable component architecture is part of how SLATE argues its own competence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML and accessible interaction patterns are required, not optional. Focus states visible, interactive elements keyboard-reachable, color contrast AA or better, motion reduced on `prefers-reduced-motion`, and touch targets sized for mobile use. No product-specific audience requirement beyond this was confirmed.
